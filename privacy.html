Proven Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Proven helps you log your GLP-1 medication and see, and document, your results. It is built so that your health information never leaves your iPhone unless you choose to share it. We don't collect any data.

What stays on your device

  • What you enter: your medication and dose history, the doses you log, weigh-ins, side-effect check-ins, pharmacy costs, labs, blood pressure, waist and body-fat readings, your insurer's reauthorization date, and an optional name and date of birth for your report.
  • Apple Health (optional): if you allow it, Proven reads weight, body fat, lean mass, waist, blood pressure, blood glucose, exercise minutes, steps and protein to show your progress and build your Renewal Report. These readings are kept in memory while the app is open. They are not copied into Proven's files. If you turn it on, Proven writes the weights and waist measurements you enter back to Apple Health.
  • Progress photos (optional): these are stored only in Proven's own storage on your iPhone. They are not saved to your photo library or iCloud.

All of this is stored only in Proven's private storage on your iPhone, using iOS data protection. The Home Screen widget gets only two values: the date of your next dose and your % lost. Backups exist only if you export one yourself. The backup file contains your health data, and the app warns you about this before it saves one.

What we do NOT do

  • No account, sign-in, server or cloud database. Proven does not sync to iCloud.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing. It is never sold, and it is never shared with anyone, including us.

The App Store privacy label for Proven is "Data Not Collected".

Sharing you choose

If you create a Renewal Report (PDF) or export a backup, you decide where it goes, for example to your clinician, your insurer, or a file location. After that, the recipient's privacy policy applies to the copy they receive.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes everything Proven stored. Deleting the app does the same. Data in Apple Health stays in Apple Health. You can manage it in the Health app.

Medical disclaimer

Proven is a personal log, not a medical device. It does not give medical advice, and it never calculates or recommends a dose. Proven is not affiliated with any drug manufacturer or insurer.
