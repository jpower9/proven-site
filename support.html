How is "% lost" calculated?

Proven starts with your baseline, which is your weight just before your first dose. It compares that with your 7-day average: the mean of your weigh-ins over the 7 days up to your latest one. Percentages are cut off at one decimal place, never rounded up, so 4.99% shows as 4.9%.

How does the renewal projection work?

Proven draws a straight line through your last 28 days of weigh-ins and extends it from your latest weigh-in to your reauthorization date. The rate is capped at 1.5% of baseline per week. This is an estimate, not a prediction. Your insurer decides using its own criteria, so talk to your prescriber early.

Where do the trial numbers come from?

From the published primary endpoints of the phase 3 trials (STEP 1, SURMOUNT-1, SURMOUNT-5, OASIS 4, ATTAIN-1, SCALE) and the FDA prescribing information. The app shows the citation and estimand for each one. These numbers are trial averages, not targets.

Apple Health isn't showing data

Open the Health app → your profile → Apps → Proven, and turn on the categories you want Proven to read. Then reopen Proven. Weigh-ins from a smart scale only appear if the scale's app writes to Apple Health.

Switching phones

Proven doesn't sync to iCloud. In Settings, choose Export backup, save the file somewhere private, and use Import backup on your new iPhone.

Restore purchases

Settings → Restore purchases. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
